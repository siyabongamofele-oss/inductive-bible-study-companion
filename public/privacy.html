Privacy notice

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Study data

This app stores studies in browser local storage on the current device. It does not create accounts or upload study libraries to an app database. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app can remove locally stored studies.

AI data

AI features are disabled by default. When enabled by the deployment, passage text, study notes, and chat messages submitted to the AI endpoint are sent to the configured Google Gemini service. Google may process that content under the applicable Gemini API terms and the deployment's paid or unpaid service tier. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information.

Exports and backups

Markdown exports and JSON backups are downloaded to a location chosen by the user. The app does not remotely delete or synchronize those files.

Hosting and logs

The hosting provider may process standard request data, including IP addresses and request metadata, for security and reliability. The operator must configure and document hosting analytics, retention, deletion, and contact procedures before public commercial launch.
